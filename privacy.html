1. Overview

Omni is a private personal AI assistant operated by Kian (the "owner") for the owner's own use. It runs on Hermes Agent (from Nous Research), installed on the owner's Mac mini. Omni is not a public service. It has no user accounts or sign-ups, and it is not offered to anyone else.

This policy explains what data Omni accesses, especially data from Google APIs, how that data is used, where it is stored and processed, who it is shared with, and how access can be revoked and data deleted.

Every Google account connected to Omni belongs to the owner. Emails and calendar events in those accounts can include information about other people, such as senders, recipients, and event attendees. Omni processes that information only as part of helping the owner, as described below.

Omni does not keep all data on the Mac mini. Its AI reasoning uses cloud-hosted models, and it communicates through cloud messaging services. These are described in sections 5 and 6.

3. How Google data is used

Omni uses Google user data only to provide user-facing features for the owner:

  • Summarizing the day's calendar events and important emails in daily briefings.
  • Finding deadlines, requests, and action items in email and reminding the owner about them.
  • Answering the owner's questions about their schedule or inbox.
  • Helping the owner plan and schedule their time, including creating calendar events.
  • Turning deadlines or tasks found in email or calendar data into entries in the owner's Notion workspace or Apple Reminders, when relevant. [REVIEW: confirm Omni does this; delete this item if not.]

Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build user profiles for any purpose other than helping the owner.

4. Limited Use disclosure

Omni's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used only to provide or improve Omni's user-facing features for the owner.
  • Google user data is transferred to third parties only as needed to provide those features (as described in sections 5 and 6), to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets with notice to the user.
  • Google user data is not used or transferred for serving advertisements, including retargeting or personalized ads.
  • Google user data is not sold, and it is not used or transferred to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person other than the owner reads Google user data, except as needed for security purposes or to comply with applicable law.

8. Sharing, selling, and advertising

  • Omni does not sell any data.
  • Omni does not use any data for advertising, and does not share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Omni does not use Google user data to train generalized AI or machine-learning models, and the owner does not train models with it.
  • Data is shared only with the services described in sections 5 and 6, and only as needed to provide Omni's features to the owner, or when required by law.

10. Revoking access

Omni's access to a Google account can be revoked at any time:

  • Go to myaccount.google.com/connections (Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services), select Omni [REVIEW: the exact app name shown on the consent screen], and choose to delete all connections.
  • Once access is revoked, Google invalidates the tokens issued to Omni for that account, and Omni can no longer read its Gmail or Calendar data.

Access to Notion, Photon, and Telegram can be revoked from each service's own settings, for example by removing the Notion integration or deleting the Telegram bot.
