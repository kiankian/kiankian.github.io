1. Overview

Omni is a private personal AI assistant operated by Kian (the "owner") for the owner's own use. It runs on Hermes Agent (from Nous Research), installed on the owner's Mac mini. Omni is not a public service. It has no user accounts or sign-ups, and it is not offered to anyone else.

This policy explains what data Omni accesses, especially data from Google APIs, how that data is used, where it is stored and processed, who it is shared with, and how access can be revoked and data deleted.

Every Google account connected to Omni is one the owner personally uses: two personal Gmail accounts and the owner's UCLA Google Workspace account. Emails and calendar events in those accounts can include information about other people, such as senders, recipients, and event attendees. Omni processes that information only as part of helping the owner, as described below.

Omni does not keep all data on the Mac mini. Its AI reasoning uses a cloud-hosted model, and it communicates through cloud services. These are described in sections 5 and 6.

2. Google user data Omni accesses

Omni connects to Google through OAuth 2.0, using two local tools on the Mac mini: the GOG command-line tool for Gmail, and Hermes Agent's Google Workspace skill for Google Calendar. Both use the same OAuth client. Google data is fetched directly from Google's APIs by these local tools. It is not fetched through any Nous-hosted connector. The owner has granted the following permissions (OAuth scopes):

  • Gmail: read-only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly

    Granted for all three connected accounts. Why: to read messages and their metadata (sender, subject, date, labels, and body) so Omni can identify important messages, deadlines, and action items. Omni cannot send, delete, or modify email with this permission.

  • Google Calendar: events on calendars the owner owns https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.owned [REVIEW: which account(s) is Calendar connected for?]

    Why: to read events so Omni can prepare daily briefings and flag conflicts, and to create events when the owner explicitly asks. This permission technically allows creating, changing, and deleting events on calendars the owner owns. Omni's rule is to create events only on the owner's explicit request, and to ask the owner before changing or deleting any existing event.

  • Basic account identity openid, email, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email

    Requested by the Gmail tool. Why: to tell which of the owner's Google accounts a request belongs to.

Omni has not been granted access to other Google services, such as Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts, or Tasks. If Omni ever needs another permission, this policy will be updated before it is requested.

3. How Google data is used

Omni uses Google user data only to provide user-facing features for the owner:

  • Summarizing calendar events and important emails in a daily morning briefing. The briefing only reads data. It does not change anything.
  • Finding deadlines, requests, and action items in email and reminding the owner about them.
  • Answering the owner's questions about their schedule or inbox.
  • Creating calendar events when the owner asks.
  • Recording academic tasks and deadlines found in email in the owner's Notion workspace, and creating Apple Reminders when the owner asks. Changing an official deadline or an existing calendar event requires the owner's approval.

Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles for any purpose other than helping the owner.

5. AI model provider

Omni's reasoning uses a cloud-hosted language model accessed through Nous Portal (Nous Research). To summarize email, interpret calendar events, or answer the owner's questions, Omni sends the relevant content to the model as part of its prompts. That content can include excerpts of Gmail messages, calendar event details, Notion content, reminders, messages, and stored memory. This processing happens on Nous's infrastructure and that of any model provider Nous uses, not on the Mac mini.

Omni currently uses Nous Portal's free tier without a signed-in Nous account, with the model stepfun/step-3.7-flash:free. Which upstream infrastructure or model provider serves this model has not been independently verified.

Nous's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy (both last updated September 28, 2026) allow Nous to store inference payloads, such as prompts and outputs, and to use them for purposes including training, unless the user enables an account setting called Privacy Mode. Privacy Mode is not enabled for Omni. Nous's Privacy Policy also says prompts and outputs may be shared with third-party model providers, which handle them under their own terms. Nous does not publish a fixed retention period for inference payloads.

As a result, content Omni sends to the model, including Google user data, may currently be stored by Nous or its model providers and may be used for AI training. 6. Other services

Omni works with the following services, each connected to the owner's own account. Each service handles data under its own privacy policy.

Notion (through Nous)

Omni reads and writes the owner's Notion workspace to organize UCLA coursework, assignments, and deadlines. This includes a Notion page called "Omni Context" that holds ongoing academic context, which may include deadlines or tasks found in email. Notion is connected through a Nous-hosted connector, so Notion content passes through Nous's connector service. Under Nous's Privacy Policy, Nous and its integration providers receive and store the access credentials for that connection. Notion stores the content on its own servers.

iMessage via Photon

Omni sends and receives iMessages with the owner through Photon's managed iMessage service. Messages, which may include briefings and summaries drawn from email or calendar data, pass through Photon's infrastructure and Apple's iMessage service. See Photon's Privacy Policy. [REVIEW: confirm this is the policy that covers the Photon service you use.] Only the owner's allowlisted contact can message Omni this way.

Telegram

Omni sends and receives messages with the owner through a Telegram bot. These messages, which may include briefings and summaries drawn from email or calendar data, are sent through and stored on Telegram's servers under Telegram's Privacy Policy. Telegram bot chats are not end-to-end encrypted. Only allowlisted users can message the bot.

Apple Reminders

Omni creates reminders on the Mac mini through a local command-line tool when the owner asks. If iCloud sync is turned on for Reminders, Apple also stores them in iCloud. [REVIEW: check System Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Reminders, then state whether sync is on.]

7. Storage and security

OAuth credentials

The Google Calendar token is stored in a file in Hermes Agent's data folder on the Mac mini, readable only by the owner's macOS user account. The Gmail tool stores its refresh tokens in the macOS Keychain. These credentials are never published, never included on this website, and never committed to any source-code repository.

Personal data and memory

Omni fetches email and calendar data from Google when it needs it. It does not keep a separate copy of the owner's mailboxes or calendars. However, excerpts and summaries of that data can persist in:

  • Omni's conversation history, stored in a local database on the Mac mini,
  • Omni's persistent memory files on the Mac mini,
  • outputs of scheduled tasks, such as the daily briefing, stored on the Mac mini,
  • application logs and local cache folders that can hold large tool outputs, on the Mac mini,
  • the owner's Notion workspace, including the "Omni Context" page, and
  • message history in Telegram and iMessage.

Safeguards

The Mac mini's disk is encrypted with FileVault. Hermes Agent asks for approval before running risky commands, and scheduled tasks automatically refuse risky commands that haven't been approved. Only allowlisted users can message Omni. No system is perfectly secure, and the owner cannot guarantee the security of third-party services.

8. Sharing, selling, and advertising

  • Omni does not sell any data.
  • Omni does not use any data for advertising, and does not share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • The owner does not train AI models with Google user data. Third-party AI providers may, as explained in section 5.
  • Data is shared only with the services described in sections 5 and 6, and only as needed to provide Omni's features to the owner, or when required by law.

9. Retention

  • Omni does not automatically delete conversation history, memory, or scheduled-task outputs. They are kept on the Mac mini until the owner deletes them.
  • Application logs rotate automatically, so older log entries are overwritten over time.
  • Data held by third parties (Nous Research, Notion, Photon, Telegram, Apple) is retained under each service's own policy. Nous does not publish a fixed retention period for inference payloads (see section 5).

10. Revoking access

Omni's access to a Google account can be revoked at any time:

  • Go to myaccount.google.com/connections (Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services), select Omni [REVIEW: confirm the app name shown there and on the consent screen is "Omni"], and remove its access. Do this for each connected account.
  • Because the Gmail and Calendar tools share one OAuth client, removing Omni's access to an account ends both Gmail and Calendar access for that account.
  • From the Mac mini, the Hermes Google Workspace skill has a revoke command that revokes the Calendar token with Google and deletes it. The Gmail tool's gog auth remove command deletes a stored token from the Mac mini. Its documentation does not say whether this also revokes access with Google, so access should also be removed from the Google Account page above.

Access to Notion can be revoked by disconnecting the Notion connector in Hermes or removing the integration in Notion. Photon and Telegram access can be revoked from each service, for example by deleting the Telegram bot.

11. Deleting data

To delete data Omni has stored:

  • OAuth tokens: revoke access as described in section 10, which also deletes the Calendar token file from the Mac mini.
  • Persistent memory: edit or delete Omni's memory files on the Mac mini.
  • Conversation history: delete individual sessions, or prune old ones, with Hermes Agent's session commands.
  • Scheduled-task outputs, logs, and caches: delete the corresponding folders in Hermes Agent's data folder on the Mac mini.
  • Notion: delete the relevant pages, including "Omni Context", in Notion.
  • Messages: delete chats in Telegram and Messages. Copies held by Telegram, Photon, or Apple are subject to their policies.
  • Nous Research: content already sent to the model may have been retained by Nous or its model providers, and the owner may not be able to delete it. Requests can be sent to Nous at privacy@nousresearch.com.

Anyone whose information may have been processed by Omni (for example, someone who emailed the owner) can ask for it to be deleted using the contact details below. The owner will delete what they control, but cannot guarantee deletion of copies held by third parties.
