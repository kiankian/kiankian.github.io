Omni — Personal AI Assistant

Omni is a personal AI assistant that helps its owner keep track of schoolwork, calendar events, important email, reminders, and messages, and sends daily briefings and deadline reminders.

Read the Privacy Policy Contact

A private, self-hosted assistant

Omni is built and used by one person for their own personal use. It is not a commercial product, it does not offer accounts or sign-ups, and it is not available to anyone else. This website exists to describe what Omni does and how it handles data.

Omni runs on Hermes Agent (from Nous Research), installed on the owner's Mac mini. The agent, its integrations, and some data processing run locally on that machine. Omni's language-model reasoning, however, uses cloud-hosted models provided through Nous Research, and Omni communicates through cloud messaging services. So not all data stays on the Mac mini. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is sent where.

Integrations and capabilities

Every integration is connected to the owner's own accounts, and everything Omni does is for the owner.

Notion

Schoolwork organization

Keeps UCLA coursework, assignments, due dates, and academic context organized in the owner's Notion workspace.

Google Calendar

Schedule awareness

Reviews upcoming events to summarize the day, and creates events when the owner asks.

Gmail

Inbox triage

Reads the owner's authorized Gmail accounts to surface important messages, deadlines, and action items.

Apple Reminders

Reminders

Creates reminders on the Mac mini when the owner asks.

iMessage & Telegram

Messaging

Sends and receives messages with the owner through iMessage (via Photon) and Telegram.

Briefings & memory

Proactive help

Sends daily briefings and deadline reminders, and keeps a persistent memory so it has the context it needs over time.

How Omni uses Google Calendar and Gmail

The owner connects their own Google accounts to Omni through Google's standard OAuth consent screen. Omni uses that access only to help the owner:

  • Google Calendar: Omni reads events to build daily briefings and warn about conflicts. It creates events only when the owner asks, and asks the owner before changing or deleting an existing event.
  • Gmail: Omni reads messages to find what matters, such as deadlines, requests, and action items, and summarizes them for the owner. Gmail access is read-only: Omni cannot send, delete, or modify email.

Relevant parts of Calendar and Gmail data are sent to a cloud-hosted AI model through Nous Portal so Omni can understand and summarize them, and summaries may be delivered to the owner by iMessage or Telegram. Google data is never sold and never used for advertising. Under the AI provider's current terms, content sent to the model may be stored and used for training.
