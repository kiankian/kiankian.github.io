1. About Omni

Omni is a private personal AI assistant operated by Kian (the "owner") for the owner's own use. It is self-hosted on the owner's Mac mini and powered by Hermes Agent from Nous Research. Its language-model reasoning uses a cloud-hosted model accessed through Nous Portal.

Omni is not a commercial product or a publicly offered service. It has no accounts, sign-ups, subscriptions, or fees. These terms describe how Omni is meant to be used and its limitations.

2. Who may use Omni

Omni is used only by the owner, with accounts that belong to the owner. Access is not offered to anyone else, and no one else is authorized to connect accounts to Omni or use its OAuth client.

3. Intended use

Omni is intended for personal organization and productivity, including:

  • organizing UCLA schoolwork and assignments in Notion,
  • reviewing Google Calendar events and creating events when asked,
  • identifying important messages, deadlines, and action items in authorized Gmail accounts,
  • creating Apple Reminders when asked,
  • sending and receiving messages with the owner through iMessage (via Photon) and Telegram, and
  • providing daily briefings and deadline reminders.

Omni must not be used for any unlawful purpose, to access accounts or data without authorization, or in ways that violate the terms of the services it connects to.

4. Third-party services

Omni depends on services operated by other companies, including Google (Gmail and Google Calendar), Nous Research, Notion, Photon, Telegram, and Apple. Use of each service is subject to that provider's own terms and policies. Omni is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

Omni's use of Google APIs is subject to the Google APIs Terms of Service and the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The Privacy Policy explains how data moves between Omni and these services.

5. AI-generated content

Omni's summaries, briefings, reminders, and suggestions are generated by AI models. They can be inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date. For example, Omni might misread an email, miss a deadline, or misstate an event time.

  • Check important information, such as due dates, exam times, and meeting details, against the original source (the email, calendar event, or course site).
  • Omni's output is not professional advice of any kind, including legal, medical, financial, or academic advice.
  • Following your school's academic policies remains your responsibility, including when you use Omni to organize coursework.

6. User responsibilities

The owner, as Omni's only user, is responsible for:

  • connecting only accounts they own or are authorized to use,
  • keeping OAuth credentials, API keys, and tokens private, and never publishing them or committing them to a repository,
  • keeping the Mac mini and connected accounts secure,
  • reviewing any action Omni takes on their behalf, such as creating calendar events, reminders, or Notion entries, and
  • following the terms of every connected service.

7. Availability

Omni is a personal project with no guaranteed uptime. It may stop working when the Mac mini is offline, a network or third-party service has an outage, access tokens expire or are revoked, or a provider changes its API. The owner may change, pause, or discontinue Omni at any time.

8. Privacy

The Privacy Policy explains what data Omni accesses, how it is used, stored, and shared, and how to revoke access or delete it.
